# HM Planning Poker — Product Context

register: product

## Product purpose
Disposable, real-time planning-poker rooms for agile estimation. Create a room, share the
link, everyone votes on a story estimate, votes stay hidden until all have voted (or an
admin reveals), then the room discusses outliers. No login, no persistence beyond the
session. Speed and zero friction are the whole point.

## Users
Primarily Human Made staff — engineers, PMs, designers — running sprint estimation on a
call or asynchronously. Sometimes external collaborators/clients join a session. Mixed
desktop and laptop, occasional tablet. They open it for 20 minutes during a planning
meeting, not all day.

## Brand & tone
Human Made: warm, human, quietly playful, competent without being corporate. The brand
mark is a red script "Hm" monogram; the accent colour is a confident red. Personality
shows through small touches: joke participant names (Star Trek / Pokémon), confetti on a
unanimous vote. Never sterile, never loud.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (sidebar + stat cards + gradient accents).
- AI-slop: identical card grids, monochrome flatness, hero-metric templates.
- Over-serious enterprise tools. This is a quick, slightly fun team ritual.

## Strategic principles
- Friction-free: no login; the create-and-share-link path is one click.
- Legible state at a glance: who has voted, who hasn't, who's observing, what the result is.
- Anti-coercion: individual votes never visible until reveal.
- Works in light and dark; respects reduced motion.
- Identity persists across refresh (same name + colour).
